# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

Be respectful, constructive, patient, and mindful that technical disagreement is not personal disagreement. Accept responsibility, apologise when appropriate, and focus on what is best for users and the community.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, sexualised attention, personal attacks, doxxing, or publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), marking the report as a conduct concern. The maintainer will review reports fairly and may edit or remove contributions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants. The privacy and safety of reporters will be protected as far as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
